Zare ዛሬ Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short

  • Zare collects no personal information and has no accounts.
  • Your settings stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app doesn't use the internet.

About this policy

This policy explains how Zare (“the app”), an Ethiopian calendar with home-screen widgets for Android, handles information. It applies to the app as published on Google Play by its developer (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

None. Zare does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It does not ask you to sign in, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Information kept on your device

To remember how you like the calendar to look, Zare saves a few preferences on your phone only:

  • language, numerals (Arabic or Ge’ez) and month-name style
  • appearance (light, dark or matching the phone) and accent colour
  • widget background and whether holidays are highlighted

These never leave your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes them.

To match your phone, Zare reads your wallpaper’s colours and the system accent colour, and shows your wallpaper behind the widget preview. This happens on the device; the wallpaper is never copied, saved or sent anywhere.

Permissions

Zare does not request the internet permission or any permission that needs your approval. The system library that keeps its widgets up to date declares a few background permissions, used only for that:

  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to keep widgets current after the phone restarts
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE to finish a widget update reliably
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to check, not use, the connection when scheduling that work

Google Play

Zare is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. We don’t receive personal information about you from Google.

If you choose to rate or share the app from its settings, Zare opens Google Play or your phone’s share sheet; anything you post or send there is handled by those services.

Children

Zare is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches you.
